ABOUT THE PROJECT

About Heart Disease Prediction

What is heart disease?

Heart disease is a broad term for conditions affecting the heart. Risk factors vary, and a website prediction cannot determine whether someone has a condition.

How This Website Works

  1. 1 Enter health information in the form.
  2. 2 Prepare the data to match model training.
  3. 3 Display the trained model's prediction.
